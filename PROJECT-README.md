Personal Finance Tracker

Description
This application is a easy, user friendly tool to help you manage your personal finances. It allows you to track your income and expenses, categorize them, and see a summary of your finances.

It is designed to be smooth to use, so you can quickly get a handle on where your money is coming from and where it is going, also to let you kmow if your expenses is greater than income which is a tool for saving knowledge.

Features
Below is what you can do with the Personal Finance Tracker application:

Your Record Transactions: Easily add your income and expenses. For each transaction, you can record:

The type of input: Is it income or an expense, depends on what you want

Amount: How much money was involved

Category: What kind of income/expense 
is it (eample, "Salary", "Food", "Transportation")?

Notes: A short description or reminder about the transaction.

Categorize Transactions: Transactions are organized into categories to help you understand your spending habits. The app comes with some default categories:

Income: Salary, Bonus, Other

Expenses: Food, Housing, Transport, Other

View Transactions: See a list of all the transactions you've entered.

Financial Summary: Get a quick overview of your finances:

Total Income: The sum of all your income.

Total Expenses: The sum of all your expenses.

Net Balance: The difference between your total income and total expenses. This shows you whether you're making or losing money.

How to Use
Add a Transaction:

Select whether it's an "Expense" or "Income".

Enter the amount of the transaction.

Choose a category from the dropdown menu.

Add any relevant notes (optional).

Click the "Add" button.

View Transactions: All entered transactions are displayed in a list.

See Your Summary: The "Summary" section shows your total income, total expenses, and net balance.

Technical Details (For the Curious)
Built with React: The application is built using React, a popular JavaScript library for creating user interfaces.

State Management: The application uses React's useState hook to manage the data (transactions, etc.).

Simple and Lightweight: This is a small, self-contained application.
